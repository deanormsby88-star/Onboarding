## Measure counts and the five-minute promise

Every template holds 12-13 measures. At roughly 20 seconds per measure that
is a four-to-five-minute check-in, which matches the commitment made to the
team. Anything above 13 breaks that promise and the cadence will start
slipping around week six. **13 is a hard ceiling — enforced by the template
editor's validation.**
